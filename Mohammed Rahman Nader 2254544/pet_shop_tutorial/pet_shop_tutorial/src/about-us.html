Welcome to Pete's Pet Shop

Pete's Pet Shop is an online platform made to offer a smooth and engaging experience for discovering pets available for adoption. Our mission is to simplify pet adoption through a visually friendly and intuitive interface.

Our Mission

We strive to connect people with their future companions in a way that is simple, informative, and enjoyable. Our tools are designed to help users find pets easily through searching or browsing with ease.

What We Offer

  • Detailed pet cards with breed, age, and location info
  • Quick search by pet name, breed, or location
  • Breed filtering dropdown to narrow down results
  • Modern responsive layout for accessibility

🐾 We aim to create a platform that feels welcoming to all users, whether you're actively searching for a pet or casually exploring.
